# Verification-Chain-Builder - Human Guide

Chain claims to checks: every assertion gets a verification step.. This guide explains what it does, when to reach for it, and how to
tell it worked.

## What this skill does

Makes confidence auditable: every claim in a document gets paired with the specific check that proves it, and those checks actually run.

## When to use it

Any consequential report or plan. Triggers: verify claims, evidence, audit trail.

## When NOT to use it

Casual brainstorming doesn't need chains. Don't build theater-chains for claims nobody will act on.

## How you know it worked

Someone else runs your checks cold and lands on your conclusions.
