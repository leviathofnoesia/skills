# Decision-Record-Mini - Human Guide

Lightweight ADRs: decision, options considered, why, revert path.. This guide explains what it does, when to reach for it, and how to
tell it worked.

## What this skill does

Lightweight decision records — under 200 words each — capturing why a choice was made, what evidence would reverse it, and how to actually go back.

## When to use it

Decisions people will relitigate later. Triggers: ADR, decision record, why did we choose.

## When NOT to use it

Not for reversible trivia or preference calls. Don't let format bloat — if it's over a page, cut scope.

## How you know it worked

Six months later, someone asks 'why X?' and gets an answer in one file read.
