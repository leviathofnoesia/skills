# Instruction-Decompiler - Human Guide

Rewrite vague requests into explicit, checkable instructions.. This guide explains what it does, when to reach for it, and how to
tell it worked.

## What this skill does

Converts fuzzy requests into precise, checkable instructions by surfacing real goals, hidden constraints, and a concrete definition of done — confirmed before work starts.

## When to use it

Requests like 'improve it' that invite misalignment. Triggers: clarify requirements, vague ask.

## When NOT to use it

When speed matters more than alignment, draft-and-show beats interview. Don't decompile trivial asks.

## How you know it worked

Zero mid-course corrections needed; requester recognizes their intent in your rewrite.
