# Self-Critique-Loop - Human Guide

Adversarially review own output against the original ask.. This guide explains what it does, when to reach for it, and how to
tell it worked.

## What this skill does

Adversarial self-review before shipping: restate the original ask, attack your own output against it, fix what's found, and disclose what remains.

## When to use it

High-stakes deliverables without available reviewers. Triggers: review my work, quality pass, critique.

## When NOT to use it

Two passes max — beyond that, seek external eyes instead. Not for casual outputs.

## How you know it worked

The limitations section names real weaknesses; recipients stop finding the basics wrong.
