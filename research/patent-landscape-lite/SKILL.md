# Patent-Landscape-Lite

Sketch the patent landscape around a mechanism or domain..

## When to Use

Before investing in a mechanism/feature, checking whether the space is patent-crowded.

## Workflow

1. Define the mechanism precisely enough to search: what does it DO, technically?
2. Search granted patents + applications across relevant jurisdictions (not just US).
3. Classify findings: active patents covering your approach / expired prior art / adjacent-but-different.
4. Read independent claims of the closest hits — they define actual scope.
5. Output risk map: clear / gray / blocked zones with specific patent numbers.

## Pitfalls

Searching product names instead of technical mechanisms. Reading abstracts only (claims define scope). Assuming pending applications are granted. Treating 'no results' as clearance (search terms were wrong).

## Verification

Closest-hit claims read line-by-line with element-by-element comparison to your mechanism documented.

## Inputs

- Task context: mechanism description, patent database access

## Outputs

- landscape map with claim-level comparisons
