# Patent-Landscape-Lite - Human Guide

Sketch the patent landscape around a mechanism or domain.. This guide explains what it does, when to reach for it, and how to
tell it worked.

## What this skill does

A first-pass survey of whether your technical approach collides with existing patents — searching by mechanism, reading actual claims, mapping risk zones honestly.

## When to use it

Before significant investment in novel mechanisms. Triggers: patent search, freedom to operate, IP landscape.

## When NOT to use it

NOT legal advice — gray zones go to patent counsel. Skip for standard-practice techniques.

## How you know it worked

Every risk-zone entry includes claim-level analysis, not just title matches.
