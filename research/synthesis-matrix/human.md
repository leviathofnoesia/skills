# Synthesis-Matrix - Human Guide

Merge many sources into a claim-by-source evidence matrix.. This guide explains what it does, when to reach for it, and how to
tell it worked.

## What this skill does

Turns a pile of sources into a grid: claims down the side, sources across the top, agreement and conflict visible at a glance — revealing which conclusions rest on solid ground.

## When to use it

Research synthesis, due diligence, decision briefings. Triggers: synthesize sources, evidence table, cross-reference.

## When NOT to use it

Two sources don't need a matrix. Extraction quality bounds everything — garbage sources make beautiful garbage grids.

## How you know it worked

Random cell-checks pass; the 'contested' row names real humans who actually disagree.
