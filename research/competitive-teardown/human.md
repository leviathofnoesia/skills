# Competitive-Teardown - Human Guide

Systematic product teardown: positioning, pricing, moat, gaps.. This guide explains what it does, when to reach for it, and how to
tell it worked.

## What this skill does

A structured deep-dive into a competitor's product as experienced by customers: where they invest, where they starve, which gaps are real opportunities versus traps.

## When to use it

Market entry, lost-deal analysis, annual strategy. Triggers: competitive analysis, teardown, differentiation.

## When NOT to use it

Not an excuse to build their feature list. Skip for markets you're leaving.

## How you know it worked

Claims trace to screenshots and changelogs; the opportunity map survives a skeptic asking 'says who?'
