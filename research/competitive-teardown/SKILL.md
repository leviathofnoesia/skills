# Competitive-Teardown

Systematic product teardown: positioning, pricing, moat, gaps..

## When to Use

Entering a market, losing deals to a competitor, or planning differentiation.

## Workflow

1. Map the product surface: signup, onboarding, core loop, pricing page, offboarding.
2. Experience it as a customer would — actually use it, document friction honestly.
3. Infer strategy from evidence: who's the target (from copy/features), moat source, revenue model mechanics.
4. Read their changelog for 12 months: where are they investing? Where starved?
5. Synthesize: exploitable gaps vs strengths to avoid, with evidence per claim.

## Pitfalls

Confirming your existing strategy through selective observation. Confusing enterprise features you can't see with absence. Copying tactics without understanding the strategy that makes them work.

## Verification

Each claimed gap cites observable evidence (screenshots, pricing pages); predictions about their trajectory are written down and revisitable.

## Inputs

- Task context: competitor product access, public materials

## Outputs

- teardown report + opportunity map
