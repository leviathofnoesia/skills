# Unknown-Unknowns-Scan - Human Guide

List what the research plan is NOT covering and why that matters.. This guide explains what it does, when to reach for it, and how to
tell it worked.

## What this skill does

Hunts for what your plan doesn't know it's missing: structured probes borrowed from adjacent fields and past failures, converting vague dread into specific watch-list items.

## When to use it

Before committing major resources; at phase transitions. Triggers: blind spots, what are we missing, premortem.

## When NOT to use it

Not infinite paranoia — rank plausibility. Skip for well-trodden paths with mature playbooks.

## How you know it worked

The plan changed after scanning; mid-point rescan surfaces genuinely NEW items.
