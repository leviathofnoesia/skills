# Unknown-Unknowns-Scan

List what the research plan is NOT covering and why that matters..

## When to Use

Research plans and project briefs feel complete but history says something always surprises.

## Workflow

1. List what the plan explicitly covers (known knowns).
2. List recognized-but-unaddressed gaps (known unknowns) with why they're unaddressed.
3. For unknown unknowns, use structured probes: adjacent-field check (what do practitioners there know that we don't?), failure-postmortem scan from similar projects, pre-mortem ('it's a year later and we failed — why?').
4. Convert plausible discoveries into cheap early-warning checks.
5. Schedule the scan to repeat at milestones — new phases reveal new blind spots.

## Pitfalls

Listing 'we don't know what we don't know' as an entry (that's the problem, not an answer). Pre-mortems done once then forgotten. Blind-spot work that never changes any decision.

## Verification

The scan produced at least one concrete addition to the research/plan; a repeat scan at midpoint finds different items than the first.

## Inputs

- Task context: current plan/research scope

## Outputs

- blind-spot register + added early-warning checks
