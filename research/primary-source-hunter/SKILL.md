# Primary-Source-Hunter

Trace claims back to primary sources instead of aggregators..

## When to Use

Secondhand claims dominate a topic and precision matters (legal, medical, financial, historical).

## Workflow

1. Take the secondhand claim and strip it to its falsifiable core.
2. Trace citations recursively until reaching non-quoting origin: statute, dataset, transcript, artifact.
3. Verify the origin actually says what everyone reports (quote-level check).
4. Note drift accumulated across quoting layers — often where errors breed.
5. Cite ONLY the primary; note discrepancies found upstream for correction.

## Pitfalls

Paywalled/embargoed primaries behind aggregator summaries. Translated sources quoted via translation-of-translation. Giving up and citing the best secondary while calling it primary.

## Verification

Your citation resolves to a document you personally read that contains no further citations for THIS claim; quote matches character-for-character or differences noted.

## Inputs

- Task context: the claim, library/database access

## Outputs

- verified primary citation + drift report on intermediaries
