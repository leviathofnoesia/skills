# Primary-Source-Hunter - Human Guide

Trace claims back to primary sources instead of aggregators.. This guide explains what it does, when to reach for it, and how to
tell it worked.

## What this skill does

Refuses to trust quotes-of-quotes: traces any claim back to the original document, verifies it says what people claim, and reports how the story drifted along the way.

## When to use it

High-stakes facts: legal, medical, financial. Triggers: primary source, original study, cite properly.

## When NOT to use it

Casual conversation doesn't need archaeology. Some primaries are genuinely inaccessible — then say so explicitly rather than fake it.

## How you know it worked

You have READ the origin document yourself; every intermediary quote got compared against it.
