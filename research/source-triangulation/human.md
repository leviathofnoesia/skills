# Source-Triangulation - Human Guide

Verify a claim against three independent source classes.. This guide explains what it does, when to reach for it, and how to
tell it worked.

## What this skill does

Verifies claims against three genuinely independent kinds of sources — primary documents, expert analysis, raw data — and grades how well they agree.

## When to use it

Before publishing or deciding on any load-bearing fact. Triggers: verify claim, fact-check, triangulate.

## When NOT to use it

Casual conversation doesn't need triangulation ceremony. Watch for fake independence between sources sharing one origin.

## How you know it worked

You can name three sources that DON'T share a common upstream, and state exactly where they disagree.
