# Source-Triangulation

Verify a claim against three independent source classes..

## When to Use

Making claims destined for decisions, docs, or publication where being wrong is costly.

## Workflow

1. State the claim precisely enough to falsify.
2. Find three INDEPENDENT source classes: primary document, expert analysis, empirical data (not three blogs quoting each other).
3. Grade agreement: corroborated / partial / conflicting.
4. Chase conflicts to origin — usually a definitional mismatch or stale data.
5. Report with confidence grade and the strongest counter-evidence found.

## Pitfalls

Counting repetition as independence (echo chambers). Treating authoritative tone as authority. Discarding disconfirming sources as 'outliers' without investigation.

## Verification

Each source genuinely derives its claim independently (checked provenance); confidence grade survives a hostile reviewer's questions.

## Inputs

- Task context: claim to verify, research access

## Outputs

- triangulation report with graded confidence
