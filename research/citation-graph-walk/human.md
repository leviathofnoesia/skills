# Citation-Graph-Walk - Human Guide

Follow citation chains forward/backward to map a topic's core.. This guide explains what it does, when to reach for it, and how to
tell it worked.

## What this skill does

Follows citation trails both directions from known papers to find a field's foundation (what everything cites) and its frontier (who's criticizing it now).

## When to use it

Building deep understanding beyond surface summaries. Triggers: citations, foundational papers, who cites whom.

## When NOT to use it

Casual lookups don't need graph walks. Fields with poor citation hygiene (grey literature) need different methods.

## How you know it worked

Your anchor list overlaps heavily with expert syllabi; your critique set includes work newer than your seed papers.
