# Literature-Scan

Rapid structured scan of a field: key papers, authors, debates..

## When to Use

Entering an unfamiliar field, or refreshing knowledge on a topic that moved while you looked away.

## Workflow

1. Fix the time window and query variants; search systematically, not inspirationally.
2. Identify anchor papers: most-cited recent survey + seminal works cited by everything.
3. Extract per paper: claim, method, evidence strength, limitation acknowledged.
4. Map the debate landscape: where do credible authors disagree, and along which axis?
5. Output a structured brief: established / contested / open questions.

## Pitfalls

Reading only what's easily accessible (paywall bias). Confusing newest with best. Missing the field's known unknowns because search surfaces answers only.

## Verification

The brief's contested section cites named researchers on both sides; a domain expert would recognize the anchors as correctly chosen.

## Inputs

- Task context: topic definition, database/search access

## Outputs

- structured field brief with reading list ranked by value
