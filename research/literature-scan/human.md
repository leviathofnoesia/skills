# Literature-Scan - Human Guide

Rapid structured scan of a field: key papers, authors, debates.. This guide explains what it does, when to reach for it, and how to
tell it worked.

## What this skill does

A systematic sweep of a research field producing a map: what's established, what's contested (by whom), what remains open — with a ranked reading list.

## When to use it

New domains, due diligence, refreshing stale knowledge. Triggers: literature review, state of the art, field scan.

## When NOT to use it

Not for quick factual lookups (just search). Full systematic reviews need more rigor than this lite version.

## How you know it worked

Both sides of each live debate get named citations; nothing important is missing per someone who knows the field.
