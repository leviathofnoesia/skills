# Expert-Interview-Prep - Human Guide

Prepare interview scripts with open questions and probes.. This guide explains what it does, when to reach for it, and how to
tell it worked.

## What this skill does

Prepares interviews that experts find worth giving: questions grounded in their published positions, structured as branching trees rather than rigid lists, hunting insights that exist nowhere else.

## When to use it

User research, journalism, hiring, learning from seniors. Triggers: interview preparation, expert questions.

## When NOT to use it

Interrogations aren't interviews — drop the gotcha framing when seeking truth over quotes.

## How you know it worked

Expert never says 'as I wrote in...'; you leave with at least one thing you couldn't have gotten elsewhere.
