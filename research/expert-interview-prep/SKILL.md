# Expert-Interview-Prep

Prepare interview scripts with open questions and probes..

## When to Use

Learning from someone's expertise within limited time; avoiding wasted sessions of generic answers.

## Workflow

1. Research the expert's actual positions from public work — never ask what they've already written.
2. Draft questions in escalating specificity: context → craft → contrarian takes → predictions.
3. Lead with open questions; save closed for confirmation. One question per turn.
4. Prepare follow-up branches for likely interesting answers (interviews are trees, not lists).
5. Plan the close: referrals, reading recommendations, permission to follow up.

## Pitfalls

Questions answerable by their bio or last blog post. Double-barreled questions getting half-answers. Rigidity: missing the unplanned insight chasing the script. Interviewing for validation instead of learning.

## Verification

Zero questions the expert has answered in existing public work; transcript yields at least one insight unavailable elsewhere.

## Inputs

- Task context: expert's public work, interview slot length

## Outputs

- question tree + research brief
