# Reproducibility-Audit - Human Guide

Check whether published results can be reproduced from artifacts.. This guide explains what it does, when to reach for it, and how to
tell it worked.

## What this skill does

Stress-tests published findings before you build on them: do artifacts exist, do numbers recompute, would the methods section actually let someone repeat this?

## When to use it

Before citing load-bearing results or building products on them. Triggers: reproduce, verify study, benchmark trust.

## When NOT to use it

Full replication is a research project — this is triage. Not for results you merely mention without relying on.

## How you know it worked

The grade cites specific recomputed numbers or specific missing artifacts — never vibes.
