# Market-Sizing-Sanity

TAM/SAM/SOM estimates with stated assumptions and ranges..

## When to Use

Business plans and pitches containing TAM/SAM/SOM numbers built on wishful compounding.

## Workflow

1. Reconstruct the sizing method: top-down (percentage of market) or bottom-up (units × price)?
2. Attack top-downs: what's actually IN that headline number? Different product? Different buyer?
3. Force bottom-up: identifiable customers × realistic acquisition × honest pricing.
4. Present as ranges with stated assumptions, not point estimates.
5. Identify the assumption whose falsity most damages the number — test that first.

## Pitfalls

'1% of China' logic. Confusing total spend category with addressable product market. Compounding growth rates beyond credibility. Point estimates masquerading as precision.

## Verification

Bottom-up reconstruction arrives within order-of-magnitude of any top-down figure; every multiplier has a named evidence source.

## Inputs

- Task context: existing sizing docs, market data access

## Outputs

- sanity-checked range estimate + weakest-assumption flag
