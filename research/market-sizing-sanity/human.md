# Market-Sizing-Sanity - Human Guide

TAM/SAM/SOM estimates with stated assumptions and ranges.. This guide explains what it does, when to reach for it, and how to
tell it worked.

## What this skill does

Audits market-size claims by forcing bottom-up reconstruction against top-down hand-waving, presenting honest ranges instead of confident single numbers.

## When to use it

Pitch decks, business cases, investment memos. Triggers: TAM SAM SOM, market size, addressable market.

## When NOT to use it

Not for established markets with solid analyst coverage (buy the report). Skip pre-idea exploration entirely.

## How you know it worked

You can trace every multiplication step to a defensible source; the range survives hostile investor math.
