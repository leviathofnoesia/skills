# Survey-Question-Design

Design survey questions that avoid bias and leading frames..

## When to Use

Gathering user/stakeholder input where biased questions would produce confident garbage.

## Workflow

1. Define decisions the data must inform FIRST — questions serve decisions.
2. Draft each question; attack for: leading framing, double-barreling, social desirability pull, jargon.
3. Replace opinion-fishing with behavior questions where possible (past action > predicted action).
4. Pilot with 3-5 people thinking aloud; catch misreadings.
5. Plan analysis BEFORE fielding: what will you do with each answer? Cut what has no consumer.

## Pitfalls

Asking what people would do instead of what they did. Scale questions without labeled points. Survey length guaranteeing drop-off bias. Questions whose answers change nothing.

## Verification

Pilot participants restate each question's meaning matching intent; every question maps to a pre-written analysis plan.

## Inputs

- Task context: decision list, target respondent profile

## Outputs

- field-ready survey + analysis plan
