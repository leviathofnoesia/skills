# Survey-Question-Design - Human Guide

Design survey questions that avoid bias and leading frames.. This guide explains what it does, when to reach for it, and how to
tell it worked.

## What this skill does

Designs survey questions that survive hostile scrutiny: behavior-focused instead of prediction-fishing, pilot-tested for misreading, and every question tied to a decision it will inform.

## When to use it

Any user research or stakeholder polling. Triggers: survey design, questionnaire, user feedback.

## When NOT to use it

Not for academic-grade psychometrics. Skip when three user interviews answer it better.

## How you know it worked

Pilot users restate questions correctly; every question's analysis slot is pre-written.
