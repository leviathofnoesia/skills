# Email-Brevity-Pass - Human Guide

Compress professional email while keeping asks explicit.. This guide explains what it does, when to reach for it, and how to
tell it worked.

## What this skill does

Compresses professional email around a single discipline: the ask comes first, context serves the ask, and everything else earns its place or dies.

## When to use it

Any email requesting action. Triggers: shorter email, get to the point.

## When NOT to use it

Relationship-maintenance emails aren't efficiency targets. Sensitive conversations need warmth over compression.

## How you know it worked

A stranger reading only the first two lines knows exactly what's wanted and by when.
