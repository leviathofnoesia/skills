# Blog-Post-Outline - Human Guide

Outline posts with argument flow and evidence slots.. This guide explains what it does, when to reach for it, and how to
tell it worked.

## What this skill does

Structures blog posts as arguments rather than topic tours: thesis-driven beats with named evidence slots and an honest treatment of the best counterargument.

## When to use it

Any persuasive or technical post. Triggers: blog outline, post structure, essay planning.

## When NOT to use it

Announcements and changelog-style posts need sequence, not argument. Personal essays may deliberately resist this scaffolding.

## How you know it worked

Headings alone convey the full case; no slot says just 'add something here'.
