# Blog-Post-Outline

Outline posts with argument flow and evidence slots..

## When to Use

Insights exist but posts stall at the blank page or wander without argument flow.

## Workflow

1. State the one-sentence thesis a reader should believe afterward.
2. Identify reader's starting belief; plan the delta (what must shift, what evidence moves it).
3. Outline argument beats: each beat = claim + evidence + transition. Cut beats not serving the delta.
4. Place the strongest counterargument and your response deliberately — don't hide from it.
5. Mark evidence slots explicitly (data needed / example needed / story needed) before writing prose.

## Pitfalls

Outlines that are topic lists instead of argument sequences. Thesis statements that are topics ('about deployment') not claims ('blue-green deploys cut incident cost'). Evidence slots left as vibes until draft panic.

## Verification

Reading only headings + thesis tells the complete argument; every slot names its evidence type; the counterargument gets real treatment.

## Inputs

- Task context: topic, intended audience

## Outputs

- argument-structured outline with evidence slots
