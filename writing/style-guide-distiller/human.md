# Style-Guide-Distiller - Human Guide

Extract a project's voice rules from existing copy samples.. This guide explains what it does, when to reach for it, and how to
tell it worked.

## What this skill does

Reverse-engineers your project's actual writing voice from its best existing copy, producing rules-with-examples that let any writer sound consistent immediately.

## When to use it

Multiple people write for the same product. Triggers: style guide, brand voice, tone consistency.

## When NOT to use it

Don't distill from copy you don't actually like. Solo writers rarely need formalization yet.

## How you know it worked

A writer who never saw the codebase produces copy your team can't distinguish from native.
