# Interview-Story-Framer - Human Guide

Shape experience into STAR-format stories with metrics.. This guide explains what it does, when to reach for it, and how to
tell it worked.

## What this skill does

Shapes real experience into tight STAR-format interview stories with quantified results and honest credit — rehearsed to conversational, not robotic.

## When to use it

Interview prep at any level. Triggers: behavioral interview, tell me about a time.

## When NOT to use it

Never fabricate — framing truth is the skill, inventing is disqualifying. Skip for portfolio-based hiring flows.

## How you know it worked

Stories survive follow-up probing: details hold because they're true and you own them.
