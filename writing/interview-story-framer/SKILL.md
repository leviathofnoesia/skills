# Interview-Story-Framer

Shape experience into STAR-format stories with metrics..

## When to Use

Preparing for behavioral interviews where experience exists but stories come out rambling.

## Workflow

1. Inventory experience clusters: projects with measurable outcomes and personal agency.
2. Select stories covering competency axes (conflict, failure, leadership, technical depth) — overlap is fine, redundancy isn't.
3. Compress each to STAR with teeth: Situation (context in one breath), Task (YOUR specific mandate), Action (decisions + tradeoffs, not we-did), Result (quantified, honest about contribution).
4. Rehearse aloud; time-box each story to 90 seconds core + follow-up depth.
5. Prepare failure stories honestly — interviewers probe for manufactured perfection.

## Pitfalls

We-stories hiding individual contribution. Results without numbers ('improved performance'). Stories rehearsed into lifelessness. Claiming sole credit for team work (experienced interviewers detect this instantly).

## Verification

Each story lands under 2 minutes with a number in the result; a listener can retell the story's point accurately afterward.

## Inputs

- Task context: career history, target role competencies

## Outputs

- story bank mapped to competency axes
