# Terminology-Consistency - Human Guide

Enforce one term per concept across a doc set.. This guide explains what it does, when to reach for it, and how to
tell it worked.

## What this skill does

Picks ONE word per concept across your entire product surface, sweeps everything else into compliance, and installs a guard so drift stays dead.

## When to use it

Multi-author docs, i18n prep, post-merger vocabulary chaos. Triggers: consistent terminology, naming standards.

## When NOT to use it

Domain-specific synonyms may be load-bearing (SQL 'index' vs search 'index'). Don't unify what users already distinguish.

## How you know it worked

Synonym scan finds zero unjustified variants; new docs naturally cite the glossary.
