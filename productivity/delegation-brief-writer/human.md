# Delegation-Brief-Writer - Human Guide

Write delegation briefs with done-states and acceptance criteria.. This guide explains what it does, when to reach for it, and how to
tell it worked.

## What this skill does

Writes delegation briefs that survive contact with reality: observable done-state, explicit constraints, context links, and acceptance criteria shared before work starts.

## When to use it

Handing off anything non-trivial. Triggers: delegate, hand off, assignment.

## When NOT to use it

Two-minute favors don't need briefs. Development assignments may deliberately withhold detail for learning.

## How you know it worked

First delivery lands close to intended; no 'oh I didn't know you wanted THAT' conversations.
