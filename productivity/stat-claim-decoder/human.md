# Stat-Claim-Decoder - Human Guide

Decode headline statistics into their honest strength.. This guide explains what it does, when to reach for it, and how to
tell it worked.

## What this skill does

Translates impressive-sounding statistics into their honest strength: finds the denominator, the baseline, the selection effects — then restates the claim at what the evidence actually supports.

## When to use it

Vendor claims, press releases, benchmark wars, internal reports. Triggers: statistic, percentage claim, study says.

## When NOT to use it

Don't decode statistics you're not acting on. Friendly sharing of interesting numbers isn't a hearing.

## How you know it worked

Your restatement gets quoted instead of the original headline; authors concede the framing is fair.
