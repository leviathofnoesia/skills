# Focus-Block-Architect

Build defended deep-work blocks around real energy peaks..

## When to Use

Deep work keeps losing to fragmentation; important work happens in scraps between meetings.

## Workflow

1. Identify your cognitively expensive work; it needs 90+ minute uninterrupted blocks.
2. Place blocks at your actual energy peak (audit when you naturally focus best).
3. Defend structurally: status set, notifications off, calendar marked busy, location changed if possible.
4. Prepare entry ramp: block starts with written next-action (no cold-start decision).
5. Review hit rate weekly; chronic breakage signals negotiation needed upstream, not more willpower.

## Pitfalls

Scheduling focus during personal energy troughs. Blocks treated as available slots by colleagues (defense failed structurally). Using the block for reactive work once something urgent appears (urgency hijack).

## Verification

Hit rate above 70% sustained monthly; one meaningful artifact per block minimum; energy levels tracked improving.

## Inputs

- Task context: task list, calendar control

## Outputs

- defended recurring blocks + artifact output
