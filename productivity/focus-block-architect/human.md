# Focus-Block-Architect - Human Guide

Build defended deep-work blocks around real energy peaks.. This guide explains what it does, when to reach for it, and how to
tell it worked.

## What this skill does

Builds defended deep-work blocks around your real energy peaks — structural defenses, prepared entry ramps, and a weekly honesty check on whether the blocks survive.

## When to use it

Cognitively demanding work keeps losing to interruptions. Triggers: deep work, focus time, maker schedule.

## When NOT to use it

Reactive roles (support, ops) may need different architecture entirely. Don't fake it with 25-minute fragments.

## How you know it worked

Blocks produce finished artifacts weekly; hit rate survives contact with real colleagues.
