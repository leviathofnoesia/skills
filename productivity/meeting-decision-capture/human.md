# Meeting-Decision-Capture - Human Guide

Capture meeting decisions with owners before memories diverge.. This guide explains what it does, when to reach for it, and how to
tell it worked.

## What this skill does

Turns meetings from talk-shows into decision factories: a running log of what was decided, who owns it, by when — published before memories diverge.

## When to use it

Any recurring meeting where topics keep coming back. Triggers: meeting notes, action items, decisions.

## When NOT to use it

Brainstorms need capture freedom, not structure. One-on-ones usually need less ceremony.

## How you know it worked

Decided topics stop returning; the log answers 'why did we choose X' weeks later.
