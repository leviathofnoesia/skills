# Weekly-Review-Loop - Human Guide

Fixed weekly ritual: wins, misses, one process experiment.. This guide explains what it does, when to reach for it, and how to
tell it worked.

## What this skill does

A fixed half-hour ritual: what worked, what didn't, one small process experiment for next week, and a clean deck for Monday — logged so patterns surface over months.

## When to use it

Every Friday. Triggers: weekly review, retrospective, week wrap-up.

## When NOT to use it

Daily standups cover short-cycle teams already. Don't expand into monthly planning theater.

## How you know it worked

Quarterly, the log shows experiments tried AND adopted — the loop closing visibly.
