# Negotiation-Prep-Sheet

One-page negotiation prep: targets, BATIMA, tradeables..

## When to Use

Salary, vendor, scope, or resource negotiations where winging it costs real money.

## Workflow

1. Define target, walk-away, and BATIMA (best alternative if no agreement) BEFORE any conversation.
2. Research counterpart's constraints and incentives — what do THEY need to say yes internally?
3. List tradeables beyond money: timeline, scope, risk distribution, publicity, future consideration.
4. Script opening frame and response to likely first offers (first reaction anchors everything).
5. Plan the pause: silence after their number, never instant counter.

## Pitfalls

Negotiating against yourself (revealing range floor). BATIMA undefined so any deal looks acceptable. Treating it as combat rather than joint problem-solving (long-term relationships suffer). Preparing positions instead of interests.

## Verification

Outcome beats walk-away; relationship intact per follow-up; preparation sheet predicted at least one counterpart move correctly.

## Inputs

- Task context: negotiation context, counterpart research

## Outputs

- one-page prep sheet used in-session
