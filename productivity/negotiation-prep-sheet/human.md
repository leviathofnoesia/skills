# Negotiation-Prep-Sheet - Human Guide

One-page negotiation prep: targets, BATIMA, tradeables.. This guide explains what it does, when to reach for it, and how to
tell it worked.

## What this skill does

One-page negotiation prep: your numbers defined before emotions run, counterpart's internal constraints researched, tradeables listed, and scripted responses to anchoring moves.

## When to use it

Salary talks, vendor contracts, scope negotiations. Triggers: negotiate, salary discussion, contract terms.

## When NOT to use it

Genuine collaborations on shared goals don't need adversarial prep. Tiny stakes don't justify the sheet.

## How you know it worked

You stayed above walk-away; counterpart moved from their opener; nobody feels mugged.
