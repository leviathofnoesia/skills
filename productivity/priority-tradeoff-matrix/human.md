# Priority-Tradeoff-Matrix - Human Guide

Weighted prioritization with axes agreed before scoring.. This guide explains what it does, when to reach for it, and how to
tell it worked.

## What this skill does

Forces priority debates into a weighted matrix where axes are agreed FIRST, scores are honest ranges, and the published rationale builds trust for next round.

## When to use it

Roadmap conflicts, sprint planning wars, resource fights. Triggers: prioritize, tradeoff, what first.

## When NOT to use it

Two equally-vital items need a conversation, not a spreadsheet. Don't matrix what a coin flip settles.

## How you know it worked

Method survives contact with disappointed stakeholders; next round runs faster.
