# Calendar-Priority-Audit

Compare actual calendar hours against claimed priorities..

## When to Use

Stated priorities and actual time allocation have quietly diverged.

## Workflow

1. Export 4 weeks of calendar; categorize blocks by stated priority areas.
2. Compute hours-per-area vs priority ranking you claim to hold.
3. Flag the three biggest gaps; identify recurring meetings consuming priority time.
4. Renegotiate or decline: each gap gets a concrete calendar change, not an intention.
5. Re-audit monthly; drift recurs silently otherwise.

## Pitfalls

Auditing during an unrepresentative week (launch week lies). Counting meeting hours but ignoring meeting prep spillover. Fixing allocation on paper while declining nothing real.

## Verification

Next month's audit shows measured movement toward claimed priorities; at least one recurring meeting died or shrank.

## Inputs

- Task context: calendar export, current priority list

## Outputs

- gap analysis + renegotiated commitments
