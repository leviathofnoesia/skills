# Calendar-Priority-Audit - Human Guide

Compare actual calendar hours against claimed priorities.. This guide explains what it does, when to reach for it, and how to
tell it worked.

## What this skill does

Compares your calendar against your claimed priorities and forces the mismatch visible: which recurring meetings eat your important work, and what specifically changes.

## When to use it

Quarterly, or when busy-but-unproductive feelings persist. Triggers: time management, calendar review.

## When NOT to use it

Not for genuinely constrained periods where survival scheduling is correct. Skip guilt-driven audits during crisis weeks.

## How you know it worked

Four weeks later, the hours actually moved toward the priorities you say matter.
