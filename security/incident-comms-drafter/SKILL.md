# Incident-Comms-Drafter

Incident communications: fast ack, kept cadence, facts only..

## When to Use

Incidents in progress or just resolved needing stakeholder communication under pressure.

## Workflow

1. First comms within 15 minutes even if empty: 'we know, we're on it, next update at HH:MM.'
2. Update cadence promised and KEPT (broken promises compound damage).
3. Facts only in-flight: what's affected, what we're doing, when next update. NO speculation on cause.
4. Post-resolution: plain-language explanation, duration, user impact quantified, prevention status linked.
5. Match channel to audience: status page for users, detailed doc for enterprise customers, internal retro separate.

## Pitfalls

Silence breeding rumor-filling. Root-cause speculation during firefighting (later proven wrong, quoted forever). Blame leaking into public comms. Over-promising resolution times under pressure.

## Verification

Stakeholders report feeling informed (survey or sentiment); update timestamps match promised cadence; final notice contains zero claims later retracted.

## Inputs

- Task context: live incident facts, stakeholder map

## Outputs

- timely comms series + final summary
